Lodi, California

Buying and selling in Lodi

Lodi draws people who want a smaller, slower town with real downtown character and wine country on the doorstep. It also has a property mix that catches buyers out: older homes near downtown, newer subdivisions on the edges, and acreage and land parcels out toward Woodbridge, Acampo and Clements.

Lance McHan is a Lodi Association of Realtors Masters Club 2025 Life Member and works throughout the Lodi area with homes, acreage, land and commercial property.

Selling your Lodi home

Selling a Lodi home or an acreage parcel needs pricing that reflects what the land and improvements actually are, not a per-square-foot average borrowed from Stockton. Lance will value your address specifically and tell you what is worth doing before it lists.

Buying in Lodi

Buying in Lodi can mean a walkable home near downtown, a newer build with a garage big enough for the toys, or several acres with room for animals. Tell Lance which version you want and he will bring you current options.

Areas Lance works in around Lodi: downtown Lodi, Woodbridge, Acampo, Clements, Lockeford and the surrounding vineyard acreage.
