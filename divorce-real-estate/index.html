Why a CDRE

A divorce home sale is not a normal home sale

There are two sets of opinions, often two sets of attorneys, sometimes a judge, and a house in the middle of it. The agent who handles it needs to be neutral, organised and able to document everything — not just good at marketing.

Lance McHan is a Certified Divorce Real Estate Expert with Cornerstone Real Estate Group in Stockton. He works with families across San Joaquin, Stanislaus and Sacramento counties on home sales where the divorce process is part of the picture.

What that looks like in practice

  • Clear, equal communication with both parties and their attorneys
  • A documented, defensible view of what the property is worth today
  • Advice on preparing and showing a home that two people may still be living in
  • Marketing and negotiation handled without taking sides
  • Escrow and closing coordinated around the legal timeline

Questions

Divorce real estate, answered

It is a specialist designation for REALTORS® who handle divorce-related property sales. Lance McHan is a Certified Divorce Real Estate Expert, which means the communication, documentation and neutrality these sales demand are part of how he works, not an afterthought.

Lance is used to transactions where two parties have to agree, and to keeping both sides equally informed. Your attorneys will advise on representation; Lance focuses on handling the property professionally and transparently.

Court-involved sales come with extra documentation and timing requirements. That is exactly the kind of sale the CDRE designation prepares an agent for.
