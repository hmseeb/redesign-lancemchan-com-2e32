How Lance helps buyers

You get a plan before you get a tour

Buying in the Central Valley goes better when the budget, the search and the offer strategy are settled up front.

Step one

Know your number

Work out what a monthly payment actually looks like at different prices, down payments and terms before you fall for a house. Use the calculator below.

Step two

Get your financing lined up

Lance walks you through what lenders will ask for and what pre-approval means so your offer is taken seriously. See financing basics.

Step three

Search on your terms

Tell Lance the cities, must-haves and budget. He sends current options — homes, acreage, land, commercial or a 55+ community home.

Step four

Tour without pressure

See what fits, ask questions, and hear the honest version of what a property needs. Check open houses too.

Step five

Write an offer that works

Price is one piece. Terms, timing and contingencies decide a lot of Central Valley deals, and that is where having Lance matters.

Step six

Close with one point of contact

Inspections, appraisal, disclosures and escrow — handled, with Lance reachable 24/7 start to finish.
