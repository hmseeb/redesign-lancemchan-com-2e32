Getting ready

Six steps to being ready to buy

Do these in order and the rest of the purchase gets much easier.

First

Work out a comfortable payment

Decide what you want to spend each month before a lender tells you what you could spend. The payment calculator lets you test scenarios.

Second

Talk to a licensed lender

Compare more than one. Ask about programmes you may qualify for, what the closing costs look like and how long their approval takes.

Third

Get your paperwork together

Identification, income, tax returns, bank statements and debts. Having them ready turns a two-week approval into a two-day one.

Fourth

Get a pre-approval letter

It tells sellers you are real. In a competitive situation, it is often the difference between an offer being considered and being skipped.

Fifth

Budget beyond the loan

Property taxes, insurance, HOA dues, Mello-Roos and mortgage insurance are real money every month. Factor them in from the start.

Then
