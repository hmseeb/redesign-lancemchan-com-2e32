The process

Four steps from first call to sold

No mystery, no pressure. Here is exactly how selling with Lance works.

Valuation & strategy

Lance reviews your property and your goals, then talks you through pricing and what the market is doing for homes like yours.

Prepare & present

Decide what is worth doing before you list, then present the home properly — photography, copy and a marketing plan built for the property.

Market & show

Your listing goes out to buyers and agents, showings get coordinated around your schedule, and you hear what buyers are saying.

Negotiate & close

Offers get reviewed side by side, terms get negotiated, and Lance manages inspections, disclosures and escrow through to the keys.
