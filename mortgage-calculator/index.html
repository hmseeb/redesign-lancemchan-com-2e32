Reading the result

What the calculator does and does not tell you

The monthly figure is principal and interest on the loan amount you created by subtracting the down payment from the price. It is the part of your payment that goes to the lender for the loan itself.

Your real housing cost in Stockton, Lodi, Manteca, Tracy, Modesto or Elk Grove will also include property taxes, homeowners insurance, any HOA or Mello-Roos assessment, and mortgage insurance if your down payment is small. Ask a licensed lender for a full estimate before you make decisions.
