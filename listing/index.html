How the search works

  1. You tell Lance the brief. Cities, budget, bedrooms, lot size, single level, 55+ community, acreage — whatever matters.
  2. Lance sends current options. Properties that are actually available, with his honest read on each one.
  3. You tour what is worth touring. No pressure, no filler appointments.
  4. Lance writes the offer. Price, terms and timing structured to be taken seriously.
